Platform Features

Everything in one place.
Nothing in between.

Stop context-switching between Google Docs, spreadsheets, and email threads. StrivePlan gives every student a single source of truth.

📋
Complete Academic Profile

Track courses, grades, and GPA across all four years with automatic weighted and unweighted calculations. A-G requirement tracking, AP course management, dual enrollment, and summer coursework — all in one view. Live GPA updates as you enter grades.

🎯
College Planning

Build and manage each student's college list with reach/target/likely categorization. Track application deadlines, essay requirements, and decision outcomes in real time.

🏆
Extracurricular Tracker

Log every activity, role, hours, and accomplishment. Competitive programs, community service, athletics — structured so you can build a compelling narrative for each student.

💬
Family Communications

Integrated messaging and meeting notes. Keep parent communication organized at the family level — siblings share parent records so you never duplicate data.

📁
Document Management

Shared files visible to students and families. Private consultant-only strategy notes and parent communication logs. Clean separation between what you share and what you don't.

🧭
Career & Interest Mapping

Track student interests, intended majors, and career pathways. Connect academic choices to long-term goals so your guidance stays holistic and forward-looking.
